## Stack
Static HTML/CSS/JavaScript proposed for GitHub Pages. No account or server required; browser-local persistence approved by user.
## Users
Russian-speaking user choosing meals and cooking without weighing every ingredient.
## Product Purpose
Turn the existing 1600 kcal menu into an interactive daily and weekly planner. Ten recipes per meal, four meals per day.
## Capabilities and Constraints
Simple portions alongside gram amounts; selectable recipes; local persistence; GitHub upload and a working online link requested. Daily calories approximate. No personal health profile in public source.
## Brand Commitments
Preserve the approved menu's green editorial identity, food imagery, readable recipe cards.
## Evidence on Hand
Existing PDF menus and generated food contact sheet in work/pdf. No photographs of the actual prepared servings.
